Barangay Management Information System

The Resident Profile in a Barangay serves as a central hub for managing and tracking key details about residents within a barangay. This feature allows Barangay Officials and administrators to efficiently store and access comprehensive data such as personal information, Family information, resident histories, resident education and occupation. With a user-friendly interface, the Resident Profile enables quick updates to resident contact details, emergency contacts. Additionally, it is possible to integrate with other modules in the BMIS, such as budget and projects, ensuring seamless communication and improved operational efficiency. Overall experience for both residents and Barangay managers.
